# Presentation rules

- The deck is 4:3 (10 × 7.5 in). Slides 1–11 are the original Gamma export; slides 12 onward are generated by writing slide XML into the unzipped package. Do not rebuild the whole deck with a library, and never touch slides 1–11 unless asked.
- Style: fonts "Tinos" / "Tinos Bold"; navy `1C3778`, crimson `B41837`, tints `EEF1F8` and `FBEDEF`, green `23764F` for positive status. Keep the corner image and the two bottom logos on every slide.
- Titles: 24pt bold, no-wrap, 32 characters or fewer. The user's viewer draws fonts wider than Keynote, so leave slack in every card, and keep body text at 10pt or larger (references may use 8.5–9pt).
- Content must stay inside y = 1.5–5.95 in; the page number (y 6.17 in) and the red footer rule sit below it.
- The page-number text on each slide is hard-coded. Renumber it whenever slides are added, moved or removed.
- **References is always the last slide.** New batches are inserted before it.
- Never invent results, metrics or targets. Use OULAD's published statistics or real run output; otherwise leave a clearly marked blank. Claims about the college portal must come from slide 3.
- Papers: only the eight in the References slide. Check a link or DOI before adding a paper.
- Before delivering, render every changed slide (Keynote export via `osascript`) and look for overflow, overlap and wrapped titles; validate the pptx still opens.
